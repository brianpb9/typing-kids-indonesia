⌨️ Typing practice becomes a joyful adventure with Poppu!

Poppu Typing Kids is a typing-learning app for children aged 5–6. Kids learn letters and type everyday words while they play — collecting stars, stickers, badges, and a graduation certificate!

🌟 4 PLAY MODES
• Letters A–Z — a warm-up tour of every letter
• Easy — short words for beginners
• Medium — longer-word challenges
• Hard — for little keyboard champions

📅 DAILY & WEEKLY MISSIONS
A new mission every day and every week keeps motivation high. Build a play streak and reach the top rank!

🏫 CLASSROOM MODE FOR TEACHERS
Teachers create a 4-letter class code, students join, and the leaderboard is stored LOCALLY on the device — perfect for kindergarten classrooms with no internet, accounts, or servers needed. Export results to CSV for reports.

👨‍👩‍👧 FOR PARENTS
• Parent dashboard: progress summary, typing accuracy, and play time
• Opt-in analytics stored 100% locally on the device
• A shareable graduation certificate

🔒 SAFE FOR KIDS
• NO ads — ever
• NO in-app purchases
• NO accounts or login
• NO data collection — all progress stays on the device
• Works fully OFFLINE after the first load

🗣️ BILINGUAL
Bahasa Indonesia and English — switch languages anytime. Great for early bilingual vocabulary building.

🎨 POPPU WORLD
Joined by Poppu and friends from Poppu World, with colorful illustrations, clear spoken words, and encouraging praise.

Start your child's first typing adventure today! 🌈
